# MESSHUB ERP – ENTERPRISE BANGLADESHI BACHELOR MESS MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Act as a Senior ERP Architect, Product Manager, UI/UX Designer, Business Analyst, Accountant, and Full-Stack Software Engineer.

Design and build a complete enterprise-grade Mess Management ERP platform called "MessHub ERP" specifically for Bangladesh bachelor messes, shared flats, student hostels, bachelor hostels, staff accommodations, and family boarding houses.

The system must support:

* Single Mess
* Multi-Mess Management
* Multi-Building Management
* Multi-Floor Management
* Multi-Room Management
* Multi-Branch Management
* Multi-Tenant SaaS Architecture
* Bangla and English Languages
* Mobile Responsive Design
* PWA Support
* Enterprise Accounting
* PDF Reporting
* Real-Time Dashboards

The goal is to become the most complete mess management ERP solution in Bangladesh.

---

# CORE BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The system must help mess owners and managers:

* Track meals accurately
* Manage member expenses
* Manage bazar costs
* Calculate meal rates automatically
* Manage utility bills
* Calculate individual dues
* Generate monthly settlements
* Track payments and collections
* Monitor profitability
* Track room occupancy
* Manage staff salaries
* Generate reports instantly
